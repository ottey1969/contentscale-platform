By using this tool you confirm you will comply with all applicable laws including:

• EU: GDPR + EU AI Act Article 50 — AI must be disclosed at start of call. Fines up to €20 million or 4% of global revenue.
• UK: PECR — legitimate interest required, TPS list respected, AI disclosed.
• All regions: B2B only. Never call consumers. Always honor immediate opt-out requests.

You must not remove or bypass the AI disclosure from the voicebot opening script. Calling without disclosing AI identity is illegal in the US, EU, and UK. ContentScale has built disclosure into the default script — do not remove it.

By activating the voicebot you indemnify ContentScale against all claims arising from your use.

5. You Are Not Calling on Behalf of ContentScale

You call as yourself or your own business. ContentScale is not your employer, partner, or co-caller. Do not represent ContentScale when making calls unless you have a specific written reseller agreement.

6. Compliance Mode Recommendation

We strongly recommend keeping Compliance Mode ON at all times.
