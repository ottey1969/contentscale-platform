How to check the percentage of AI-generated content for free

You can use a free AI content detector to estimate how likely a text is to have been generated by AI. Copy the text into a detector, run the check, and read its percentage or probability score. Compare the result with another tool. No detector can reliably tell you the exact percentage of words originally written by AI.

If you are checking a webpage for SEO, distinguish AI authorship estimates from content quality. ContentScale's free 100-point ContentScore measures the latter, not the percentage of AI writing.

How to use free AI content detectors

  1. Copy a representative passage of the text you want to review.
  2. Open a detector and paste your text. Check its current free allowance and privacy terms before submitting.
  3. Run the analysis and read both the overall score and any highlighted passages.
  4. Check the same passage with another detector, keeping the input identical.
  5. Review the original sources, facts, drafts and context before drawing a conclusion.

What does an AI detection percentage mean?

A percentage may represent classification confidence, an estimated fraction of flagged text, or another model-specific measure. A reported 80% score does not necessarily mean that 80% of the words were produced by AI. Human writing can be flagged, and AI-assisted writing can go undetected. ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude outputs cannot be attributed to a particular model with certainty from text alone.

Who should use an AI content checker?

  • Editors and marketers: use flagged passages as prompts to review originality, facts and clarity.
  • Teachers and students: use the result only as one signal alongside drafts and institutional policy, never as sole evidence of misconduct.
  • Website owners and SEO professionals: assess whether the page answers its audience's question, demonstrates expertise and provides reliable evidence.

Why AI content detectors are unreliable — and why ContentScale measures what actually matters

AI detectors sound authoritative. They are not. The research is clear:

  • Accuracy ranges from 61% to 90% depending on the tool — far below what vendors claim. A Sultan Qaboos University study found performance on hybrid human–AI text dropped to nearly 0%.
  • False positives are rampant. University of Maryland researchers found that minimal paraphrasing with GPT-4o defeats detection in 10%–75% of cases. The same human-written text gets different scores on the same tool run twice.
  • Non-native English writers are disproportionately flagged. Common Sense Media documented a 20% false positive rate for Black students versus 7% for White students — the detector is measuring language patterns, not AI use.
  • Google doesn't use AI detectors. Google has never used AI detection as a ranking signal. They evaluate whether content is helpful, original, and credible — not how it was written.
  • Freelance writers are losing work unfairly. Gizmodo reported in 2024 that AI detectors caused job losses among freelance writers whose human-written work was incorrectly flagged.

The right question is never "does this read as AI?" — it is "does this content demonstrate genuine expertise, cite real sources, and give readers something they cannot find anywhere else?" That is what the advanced GRAAF model measures. Robotic, undifferentiated output — regardless of which artificial intelligence tool produced it — fails that test. Why ContentScale goes beyond AI detection

AI detectors estimate authorship patterns; ContentScale assesses page quality. A detection score alone cannot tell you whether a page meets its audience’s needs or will perform well in search.

How AI detection works (perplexity and burstiness)

Some detectors use measures of text predictability and variation in sentence structure. Other detectors use trained classifiers. Human and AI-assisted writing may overlap in these patterns, so false positives and false negatives are possible.

Limitations of AI detectors

  • Detection scores are not evidence of the exact percentage of AI-written words.
  • A text may be misclassified regardless of its actual author.
  • AI assistance alone does not establish a violation of Google policy; quality and the purpose of the content matter.
  • Use human review, original sources and evidence before making editorial decisions.

Why factual accuracy matters for SEO and GEO

Invented AI answers, citations, statistics and recommendations can undermine content quality and trust. Google quality raters evaluate search quality but do not directly determine individual page rankings. Unreliable or misleading content may perform poorly when ranking systems reassess quality, including during core updates. Large-scale content created primarily to manipulate rankings may also violate Google's spam policies.

ContentScale recommends grounding editorial decisions in actual AI answers, verifiable sources and available search evidence. Missing evidence must be identified as missing, not replaced with invented findings. Verify factual claims before publication.

How ContentScale turns evidence into SEO and GEO improvements

The workflow starts with research, not assumptions. Recommendations and content should follow the available evidence, with human review before publication.

  1. Research: Review the page, search intent, Google results, competitors and available Search Console data.
  2. AI evidence: Analyse actual retrieved or supplied answers from Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude, ChatGPT Search and Microsoft Copilot, including available source references.
  3. Gap analysis: Identify relevant missing topics, entities, H2 headings and People Also Ask questions where supported by research.
  4. Evidence validation: Distinguish observed findings, unverified claims and missing evidence. A missing AI answer is not proof that a page was not cited.
  5. Recommendations: Prioritise changes supported by evidence. Do not invent findings to fill gaps.
  6. Content creation: Use the research brief and GRAAF and CRAFT criteria to draft or improve content.
  7. Human-in-the-loop: Verify factual claims, sources and proposed changes before approving publication.
  8. Proof & History: Record a baseline and compare available search performance and citation evidence after publication.

This describes the intended editorial workflow across ContentScale tools. Evidence availability and individual features depend on access, configuration and research coverage. No ranking or citation outcome is guaranteed.

International SEO checklist for content scaling

International SEO requires more than translation: local search intent, distinct language URLs and consistent technical signals.

Ten checks for international SEO

  1. Hreflang — Use reciprocal language annotations and an appropriate x-default.
  2. Market research — Choose markets using local search demand and business relevance. Review country-level Search Console performance.
  3. Structured data — Localize visible content and applicable schema properties, such as inLanguage for Article and WebPage.
  4. Keyword research — Research queries and intent independently for each language and market.
  5. Local information — Use appropriate currency, units, dates, terminology and business information.
  6. References — Use reliable local sources and earn relevant links naturally.
  7. URL structure — Use stable subdomains, subdirectories or country domains, with a self-referencing canonical for each equivalent language page.
  8. Freshness — Verify that information is accurate and relevant to each market.
  9. Natural language — Answer local conversational questions where useful.
  10. AI visibility — Research actual AI answers for each target language and market; document missing evidence.

Frequently asked questions about ContentScale and AI content

Can I check the percentage of AI-generated content for free?

Yes. Free AI detectors estimate whether text resembles AI-generated writing. Their scores do not prove authorship or establish an exact percentage of AI-written words.

Is ContentScore an AI detection percentage?

No. ContentScore measures content quality using GRAAF, CRAFT and technical SEO. It is not a measure of human or AI authorship.

Can AI detectors identify ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude reliably?

Not with certainty. Human writers and different AI models can produce similar patterns. Consider writing history, sources and human review.

Which tool should I use to improve Google rankings and AI visibility?

Use ContentScore to assess quality, Free Rank Tracker to monitor Google positions, Free Growth to research Google AI Overviews (AIO), Perplexity, Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT Search and Microsoft Copilot, and GSC Audit to review Search Console data. None guarantees rankings or citations.

What is content scale?

Content scaling is the systematic production and improvement of content at volume while maintaining quality. ContentScale helps assess that quality and prioritize improvements.

Is ContentScale the same as Content at Scale?

No. ContentScale is an independent SEO and GEO assessment tool. Content at Scale, which rebranded as BrandWell, is a separate product.

Is the ContentScore scanner free?

Yes. You can scan a single page without an account or credit card. Bulk scanning and consultancy may have separate access conditions.

What is the difference between GRAAF and CRAFT?

GRAAF assesses credibility, relevance, actionability, accuracy and freshness (50 points). CRAFT assesses editing and presentation (30 points). Technical SEO adds 20 points.

Why use GRAAF and CRAFT together?

The frameworks address complementary aspects of content quality. Together with technical SEO, they form a diagnostic ContentScore out of 100, not a guarantee of search performance.

How does ContentScale differ from AI detectors?

AI detectors estimate authorship patterns. ContentScale evaluates page quality and identifies improvement priorities. Growth separately researches visibility in Google and AI answers.

How long does traffic recovery take?

Recovery timing varies. Monitor indexing, rankings, impressions and clicks after publication. No fixed recovery window is guaranteed.
