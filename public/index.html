The GRAAF Framework is ContentScale's proprietary methodology for AI-era SEO optimization. It stands for: Genuinely Credible, Relevance, Actionability, Accuracy, and Freshness. This framework ensures your content meets Google's E-E-A-T guidelines and ranks well against AI Overviews.

Based on our work with 200+ businesses, typical SEO recovery takes 60-90 days.
