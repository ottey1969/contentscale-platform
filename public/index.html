🔐 DNS Authentication (Required Before Sending)

Without these 3 DNS records, your emails will land in spam regardless of warmup. Set these in your domain registrar (Cloudflare / GoDaddy / Namecheap).

✅ SPF Record TXT Record

Tells mail servers that SendGrid is authorised to send email on behalf of your domain.

Name: @ (or your domain)
v=spf1 include:sendgrid.net ~all
🔑 DKIM Record CNAME Record

Cryptographic signature proving emails actually came from you. Generated automatically in SendGrid — go to Settings → Sender Authentication → Authenticate Your Domain.

SendGrid generates 2 CNAME records for your domain. Copy them exactly into your DNS. Takes 24–48 hrs to propagate.
🛡️ DMARC Record TXT Record

Protects your domain from being spoofed. Tells receiving servers what to do if SPF/DKIM fail.

Name: _dmarc (or _dmarc.yourdomain.com)
v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:[email protected]

Start with p=none (monitor only). Move to p=quarantine after 2 weeks of clean reports.
