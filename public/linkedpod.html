How it works

From post to viral
in 3 steps

LinkedPod turns your network into a coordinated reach machine — without ever touching LinkedIn's automation rules.

STEP 01
📝

You post on LinkedIn

Create a boost session — paste your post text. AI generates a unique personalised comment for each member — nobody posts the same text.

›
STEP 02
🔔

Members get alerted

Everyone in the pod hears a sound alert. They open the session, pick a comment, copy it, paste on LinkedIn — from their own account.

›
STEP 03
📈

LinkedIn algorithm fires

6–15 real comments in the first hour signals quality to LinkedIn. Post gets boosted in the feed. You earn reach without buying ads.

›
STEP 04
🔄

You return the favour

Every boost earns +2 credits. Use credits to launch your own sessions. The more you give, the more you receive.
